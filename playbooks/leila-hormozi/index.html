01

Hire for the outcome

Write down what the role must achieve before you write the job post.

How

List 3-5 measurable outcomes for the first 90 days, then the traits needed to hit them.

Example (UOP-original)

A shop owner hiring an office manager writes: "invoices out within 24 h, phones answered by 3 rings" before posting.

Source: Build with Leila Hormozi, Ep. 362 “The Hiring System That Lets Your Business Run Solo” (May 21, 2026) — Apple Podcasts. Supporting: “My $100M Hiring Strategy” (Sep 2, 2023). Paraphrased.

02

Write it down (SOPs)

If a process lives only in someone's head, the business depends on that head.

How

Record the task once, write the steps, name an owner, review it after first use.

Example (UOP-original)

The weekly payroll run becomes a 9-step checklist anyone can follow.

Source: Build with Leila Hormozi, Ep. 370 “The Stage Where Most Businesses Die” (Jun 18, 2026) — episode notes. Supporting: Ep. 310 “How Systems Are The Real Secret to Success” (Aug 18, 2025). Paraphrased.

03

Clear standards, then accountability

People can't hit a bar they can't see.

How

Give each role 1-3 numbers and a definition of done; review weekly.

Example (UOP-original)

A dispatcher owns 'jobs scheduled within 1 day' and checks it every Monday.

Source: Build with Leila Hormozi, Ep. 138 “Why Accountability Creates a Culture of Success” (May 6, 2024) — Apple Podcasts; Ep. 362 operating-system section (outcomes + metrics + weekly 1:1s). Paraphrased.

04

Fast, specific feedback

Feedback is most useful soon after the moment and tied to a specific behavior.

How

Name the behavior, its impact and the expected change; follow up.

Example (UOP-original)

"The quote went out without photos; the client called twice. Next time attach them before sending."

Source: Build with Leila Hormozi, “The 5 ‘A’s to Giving Critical Feedback” (Aug 19, 2023); Ep. 312 “Why Positive Reinforcement Works Better Than Punishment” (throwback / Aug 22, 2025) — episode notes. Paraphrased.

05

Leader as bottleneck

When everything waits on the owner, the owner is the constraint.

How

List every decision only you make; hand off the repeatable ones with a written rule.

Example (UOP-original)

Refunds under $100 no longer need the owner's approval.

Source: Build with Leila Hormozi, Ep. 346 “The 5 Systems That Will Make Your Business Run Itself” (Mar 26, 2026), section “Remove the founder bottleneck” — Apple Podcasts. Paraphrased.

06

Hire slow, act on misfits

Careful hiring beats fixing a bad hire; when it clearly isn't working, decide rather than drift.

How

Use a work-sample test before offers; set a 30/60/90 check with clear criteria.

Example (UOP-original)

A paid 2-hour trial task replaces a gut-feel interview.

Source: Build with Leila Hormozi, “My $100M Hiring Strategy” (Sep 2, 2023); Ep. 43 “What I Learned About Firing Employees” (Jun 6, 2023) — iHeart; Ep. 346 enforcement matrix. Supporting: Ep. 235 onboarding 30/60/90. Paraphrased.
